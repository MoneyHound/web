ABOUT US

Building the future of compliance testing

MoneyHound was built for the teams that keep financial systems safe — compliance officers, data scientists, and AML analysts who need better tools to test, train, and validate their detection systems.

Real customer data is too risky to use in testing. Synthetic data is too simplistic to be useful. MoneyHound bridges that gap — generating production-grade transaction datasets with realistic fraud patterns, behavioral anomalies, and jurisdiction-specific AML rules baked in.

We believe compliance teams deserve the same quality of tooling as the teams they monitor. That starts with better data.
